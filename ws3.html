Attack the plan

Ask the AI to find the weak spots in its own plan. Then decide which risks are real.

Your team’s project

Saved on this phone only and shared across the four workshop pages. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Your turn · 10 minutes

  1. Rotate roles. Run the prompt in the same chat as your Workshop 2 plan, so the AI can see it.
  2. Mark 3 risks that are real for your project and 1 the AI got wrong.
  3. For each real risk, circle a response (the 4 Ts below), then write your own fallback on your A4 sheet.
  • Treatreduce how likely or how bad it is (PMI: mitigate)
  • Tolerateaccept it and keep watching (PMI: accept)
  • Transferpass it to someone else, e.g. insurance or the supplier’s contract (PMI: transfer)
  • Terminatechange the plan so it cannot happen (PMI: avoid)


Checker’s question: would this actually happen on our project? How do we know?


Before you trust the answer
AI can invent facts and references that look real. Check them.
It sounds confident when it is wrong. You are the engineer of record.
Never paste confidential, client or personal data into a public AI tool.
