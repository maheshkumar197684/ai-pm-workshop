Your turn · 12 minutes

  1. Rotate roles. New driver: run the better prompt.
  2. Rewrite 5 tasks on your A3 sheet so each passes the SMART test below.
  3. Swap sheets with the next team and check their tasks.
  • SSpecific: one clear output
  • MMeasurable: a number or a clear “done”
  • AAttainable: you have the people, parts and skills
  • RRealistic: fits around shutdowns, lead times and other work
  • TTime-bound: a date, not “Q1”


Checker’s question: could a manager tick this off on the due date?


Before you trust the answer
